1 · Select template

Both original SVG files are kept intact. New elements are placed in a separate editable layer.

2 · Add text

Click and drag the selected text in the preview. Added text is always horizontal (0° rotation).

3 · Automatic photo insertion

Supports JPG, PNG, SVG, WebP, GIF (first frame), BMP and AVIF where browser-compatible. HEIC/HEIF depend on browser support; TIFF requires conversion. Each photo produces its own exported image. The editor analyzes the actual rendered template pixels to find the largest light rectangular region. Drag inside to pan the photo, or choose Move/Resize area to adjust the detected region.

Scanning…

The insertion area is scanned from the actual SVG rendering. Drag to pan the photo. Switch to Move frame or Draw new frame to correct detection. Hold Shift while dragging to move the frame. Drag the blue corner handle to resize.

4 · Export

PNG is encoded losslessly at the SVG's native 2480 × 3508 resolution (or custom SVG dimensions). SVG retains vector content and embeds the selected photo. Fonts may vary on other devices unless converted to paths or embedded with appropriate licensing.
